About

From writing code to owning the product

I started as a developer at Philips Healthcare, building features for the CT and MR learning apps. Working close to the code taught me how architecture, APIs and engineering limits shape what a product can be.

In 2023 I moved into product ownership. Since then I have owned discovery, backlog and release readiness for a global CMS migration and an AI solution for factory operators, working with engineering, UX, QA and business teams across countries.

Outside work, I build small products end to end to practise the whole journey: find a real problem, test it with data, write the backlog, design the flows and ship something people can open on their phone. I also write about product thinking on Instagram.

How I work

The same six steps on every project

  1. DiscoverMarket scan and assumptions written down before any build.
  2. ResearchSurveys and interviews, each question tied to an assumption.
  3. DefinePersona, problem statement, vision and a North Star metric.
  4. PrioritiseEpics, user stories with acceptance criteria, MoSCoW scope.
  5. Design and buildFlows, screens and a working POC that real people can open.
  6. MeasurePilot, metrics and a persevere, pivot or narrow decision.
